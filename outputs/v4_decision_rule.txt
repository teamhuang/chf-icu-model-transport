== UPDATE DECISION RULE (suggested operational guidance from v4)==

Define per-site monitoring state from:
  - observed/expected ratio (O/E) at current site using frozen model
  - available update-set size in patients (or deaths)

Recommended decision tree:
  A) |O/E - 1| < 0.10  OR  update-set < 100 patients:
       ACTION: do not update; continue monitoring; frozen model remains acceptable
  B) |O/E - 1| >= 0.10 but < 0.20, OR update-set 100~499 patients:
       ACTION: intercept-only update (1 parameter, minimal variance, safe at small n)
  C) |O/E - 1| >= 0.20 AND update-set >= 500 patients (or ~60-80 deaths):
       ACTION: full 2-parameter logistic recalibration (slope + intercept)
  D) Full retrain (all coefficients + re-selection of variables) only if:
       CASE-MIX dramatically different (mean admission year / ICD mix shifted)
       AND update-set >= 2000 patients (enough to support > 20 events per variable)

Based on v4 empirical findings:
  - intercept-only is more stable than 2-parameter recalibration at n<200 (SD of slope ~ 0.35 to 2.06 across sizes, with intercept-only variance lower because it estimates 1 parameter).
  - 2-parameter recalibration is stable from ~500 patients.
  - reweighting diagnostics show case-mix does NOT explain calibration drift, so case-mix-only fixes (e.g., importance weighting) are ineffective; intercept / slope updates target the actual failure mode.
  - post-update DCA (independent test) shows net benefit improvement in thresholds 5%-40%.
